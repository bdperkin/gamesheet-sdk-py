# Explanation

> Explanation, or discussion, **clarifies and illuminates a particular topic**. It broadens the documentation's coverage of a topic.
>
> — [diataxis.fr/explanation](https://diataxis.fr/explanation/)

Explanation is **understanding-oriented**. It exists to broaden context — why choices were made, how the system behaves under the hood, what
assumptions constrain the design. It does not give step-by-step instructions and it does not list APIs.

## Authoring guidance

When adding an explanation to this section:

- **Free yourself from a concrete task.** Discuss the topic in its own right.
- **Connect ideas.** Cross-reference {doc}`../reference/index` for _what_ and {doc}`../how-to/index` for _how_.
- **Acknowledge trade-offs.** Explanation is the place to say "we don't do X because Y."
- **Welcome multiple perspectives.** Unlike reference, explanation can hold competing views and let the reader weigh them.
